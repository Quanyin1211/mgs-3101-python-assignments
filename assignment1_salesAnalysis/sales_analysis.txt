Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
Shop: Lower Manhattan
---Understanding of the Data---

I used sales data from March 27, 2023 to calculate daily revenue,
and I also analyzed the full dataset from January to June 2023 to support my recommendations.
I chose March because the store's sales had been steadily growing from January to June,
so I picked the middle month. Within March, I selected one day randomly. 
In Excel, I filtered by product category: I grouped Bakery as one category, and Coffee,
Tea, and Drinking Chocolate as another.
I then looked at the average quantity sold and average unit price for each group,
and calculated the daily revenue.

---Here is the data and outcome---

Drinks sold: 369
Price per drink: $2.96
Drink revenue: $1092.24

Pastries sold: 45
Price per pastry: $3.39
Pastry revenue: $152.55

Total revenue: $1244.79
Total revenue is at least $500

---Recommendation 1---

Over 6 months, the store sold 57,518 drinks but only 8,040 pastries, 
giving a pairing rate of only 14%. This means out of every 100 customers who buy coffee, 
only 14 are willing to buy a bakery item. Interestingly, the average price of pastries is 
actually higher than that of drinks. I recommend using promotions or combo deals 
- for example, a $5 combo where customers can choose any drink plus one bakery item.

---Recommendation 2---

Looking at the long-term trend, sales have been steadily increasing from January to June 
(with only a slight decrease in February). January: 8,249, February: 7,895, March: 10,209, 
April: 12,249, May: 16,175, June: 16,965. Sales grew 2.06 times from January to June. 
This means hiring more staff is necessary. 
To keep up with the growing customer traffic and demand during peak hours, 
the store should also consider adding more espresso machines and drink preparation equipment. 
Otherwise, even with more staff, a lack of machines would reduce marginal output.

---Recommendation 3---
Coffee clearly has the highest sales volume 
(18,204 records, compared to Tea at 13,912 and Bakery at 7,890). 
I recommend continuing to invest in developing new coffee drinks and positioning coffee 
as the store's main selling point.
